Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 | Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Key Privacy Highlights

  • On-Device First: Your facial photos and skin analysis computations are executed locally on your device. We do not upload your scan photos to our servers.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal health or biological data.
  • Health Connect Limited Use: Health data accessed via Android Health Connect is strictly used for displaying personal lifestyle correlations and is never transmitted off-device or shared with advertisers.

1. Introduction

529 Labs ("we", "our", or "us"), operating at 529labs.ai, provides the GlowGraph mobile application and related services. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the GlowGraph mobile application.

By using GlowGraph, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Access and Process

A. Camera and Facial Photos

GlowGraph requires access to your device camera (android.permission.CAMERA) to capture photos for skin health evaluation. All facial landmark detection, deterministic pixel analysis (hydration, redness, texture, pore visibility), and optional vision-language evaluation are executed locally on your device. Captured photos are stored in your application's private on-device sandbox. Photos are never uploaded to remote servers or shared with third parties.

B. Android Health Connect Data

With your explicit, opt-in consent, GlowGraph reads lifestyle metrics from Android Health Connect:

  • Sleep: READ_SLEEP
  • Physical Activity: READ_STEPS
  • Cycle Tracking: READ_MENSTRUATION

Purpose of Health Data Access: This data is accessed solely to correlate your daily lifestyle habits (such as sleep duration and activity levels) with your on-device skin health score trends, providing personalized self-care insights within the app.

Google Play Health Connect & Limited Use Compliance:
Our use of information received from Health Connect adheres strictly to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:
  • We only use Health Connect data to provide user-facing features that are prominent in the app's user interface.
  • We do not transfer Health Connect data to third parties, advertising platforms, or data brokers.
  • We do not use Health Connect data for user profiling, advertising, or creditworthiness determinations.
  • Health Connect data is processed on-device and is never used to train generalized AI/ML models.

C. Notifications and Alarms

  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to schedule daily reminder alerts at the specific wall-clock time you choose for your routine skin scan.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display local reminder alerts on your device.

3. How We Store and Retain Data

All scan history, skin metrics, routine tracking logs, and Health Connect correlations are saved locally in the app's SQLite / Room database on your device. We do not maintain a remote cloud copy of your biometric scans or photos.

You may delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual scans or entries within the GlowGraph app settings.
  • Clearing the app storage/cache via Android System Settings.
  • Uninstalling the GlowGraph application from your device, which automatically removes all locally stored application data.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

GlowGraph does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or tracking networks. If crash reporting is enabled in future releases, it will only transmit non-identifiable technical diagnostics (stack traces, device OS version) and will never include photos, face crops, or health records.

5. Medical and Diagnostic Disclaimer

GlowGraph and 529 Labs do not provide medical advice. GlowGraph is designed exclusively for cosmetic, lifestyle, and wellness tracking. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any dermatological disorder or health condition. Always consult a board-certified dermatologist or healthcare provider for medical concerns.

6. Children's Privacy

GlowGraph is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
